Investment Philosophy

How we think about investing

Eight principles that guide every check we write.

01

Visionary Partnership

We forge strong partnerships with founders passionate about disrupting industries and solving real-world problems with innovative tech solutions.

02

Value Beyond Capital

Strategic guidance, mentorship, and access to our network of experts and resources — we extend far beyond the check.

03

Early-Stage Focus

The seed stage is where we live. We identify and support promising startups in their nascent stages when the impact of our support is greatest.

04

Prudent Risk-Taking

We evaluate opportunities meticulously while nurturing an environment conducive to innovation, calculated risk, and growth.

05

Long-Term Vision

Our commitment goes beyond initial funding. We foster long-term relationships from concept to market disruption and beyond.

06

Ethical Practices

Integrity and trust are the cornerstones of our relationships with founders, investors, and stakeholders. We uphold the highest standards.
